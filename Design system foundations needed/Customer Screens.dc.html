Grounded in the repo
The grid card is the project's Store Card, unchanged — 4:5 image inset in the white frame, centred glass Subscribed pill, verified tick beside the name, one full-width 48px pill footer. ShopCard.jsx's category badge survives as a glass chip on the image; its heart indicator and its second "View shop" button don't, because Store Card already carries status in the pill and the whole card opens the shop. Nav is the real five destinations — dashboard, shops, scan, wallet, account — with the repo's own icons, as a bottom bar on mobile and the rail on desktop. Only disputable rows carry a flag. Earn, reversal and redeem-with-a-redemption can be flagged; adjust, expiry and suspension reversal can't, so those rows have no affordance — matching disputeArgsFor. The slug fallback is kept. When a scanner fails, staff can type the customer's code — it sits under the QR behind a divider, as in source. Name and email are read-only on the account screen; only phone and interests save.
Changed, and why
A total, then the per-shop split. The wallet opened straight into three equal balance cards with no summary. A single total at the top answers "how am I doing" in one glance; each shop card then shows progress to its nearest reward. Save only appears when something changed. A permanently active Save button teaches nothing; this one is inert until the form is dirty, then confirms. Confirmations are bottom sheets. Flagging and logging out arrive from the thumb edge on glass, per the app's overlay recipe, instead of centre-screen dialogs. The locked code state is explicit. A dashed, padlocked placeholder plus a resend button beats a one-line card that leaves you stuck.
