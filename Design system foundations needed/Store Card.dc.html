Read of the reference
Kept: portrait proportion, the image inset inside a visible card frame rather than bleeding to the edge, a floating pill over the image for status, the name with an inline verified tick, and a pill control anchored to the footer. Dropped: the description, the two stat clusters and the "Top Creator" badge — your brief bans description, ratings, counts and metadata. The footer control takes the full width the stats used to occupy, which also buys a bigger touch target. Changed: one canonical treatment instead of the reference's three (white / faded / fully tinted). Dozens of these appear in a grid, so identical instances beat variety. Subscribe is a filled teal pill — the reference's outlined control is too quiet to be the primary action here.

Glass: the status pill over the image is the card's one glass layer — rgba(255,255,255,.62) at blur 10 with a 1px white edge, text darkened to #1C3A2E to hold AA on a light photograph. The card body, name and control stay opaque; nothing else on the card blurs. Invariants: 4:5 image at every width; 8px inset frame; name truncates on one line and never wraps; the control spans the card's full inner width so its target grows with the card instead of drifting; the card declares no width of its own — the grid does. Accessibility: real <button aria-pressed> labelled "Subscribe to {name}"; subscribed carries a tick and the word, never colour alone; 2px teal :focus-visible ring at 2px offset; 44px minimum target on touch, 40px from 960px up; image gets the store name as its alt text, the fallback is aria-hidden.
