Decisions on this console
Charts are palette-only. Teal for issued, a deepened accent (#B79A00) for redeemed — the pale accent can't carry a 2px line at AA. No third hue, no gridline noise beyond three rules, every figure tabular. The phone is the till, the desktop is the office. Mobile leads with the four KPIs and the scanner one tap away; desktop drops the scanner entirely — nobody scans a customer's phone from a back-office screen — and spends the width on the same four KPIs, the two charts side by side, and the till feed as a real table. The rail navigates independently of the phone, so a colleague scanning at the till never changes what the office screen is showing. Both money actions confirm. Awarding points and pausing a live reward open the same sheet component with a before/after summary — pausing is reversible, so it confirms rather than double-confirms; deleting a reward would need typed confirmation. Liability is stated, not buried. Issued minus redeemed is outstanding points the shop owes; owners should see it next to the chart rather than compute it.
